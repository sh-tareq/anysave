1. What AnySave is for

AnySave is a personal tool for saving videos and files you are allowed to save, and for converting your own media, for example:

  • content you made or uploaded yourself
  • content whose owner has given you permission
  • content published under a licence that allows downloading (such as Creative Commons) or in the public domain
  • uses your local law allows, such as personal backup or fair use / fair dealing

6. Privacy

  • AnySave has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The author's servers receive nothing from you.
  • Downloads go directly from the website to your device, and conversions happen on your device.
  • To tell a file from a web page, AnySave may first ask that website about the link (a small request that downloads nothing).
  • Desktop: when "Catch downloads from websites" is on, AnySave sees the downloads you start in your browser (their address and file name) so it can show and sort them. This stays on your computer. Turn it off in the manager.
  • If you log in to a site (Android) or tick "Use my Chrome login" (desktop), the login cookies stay on your device. They are sent only to that same website. On desktop they are written to a temporary file that is deleted as soon as the download finishes.
  • AnySave connects to GitHub to download and update yt-dlp. The desktop installer also downloads ffmpeg (Mac: ffmpeg.martin-riedl.de, Windows: GitHub).

7. Copyright complaints

If you believe AnySave is being used to infringe your rights, contact the author. Note that AnySave hosts no content, so infringing copies are on the user's own device and the original stays on the website it came from.

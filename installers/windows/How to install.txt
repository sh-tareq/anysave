AnySave for Windows - download videos, files and convert media
==============================================================

1. Unzip first
   - Right-click the AnySave zip file and choose "Extract All...".
     (Running the installer from inside the zip won't work.)

2. Install the helper
   - Double-click "Install AnySave.bat" in the extracted folder.
   - If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click "More info",
     then "Run anyway".
   - A window shows the progress. It downloads about 130 MB.
     Wait until it says "Done!", then press any key.

3. Add the extension to Chrome (or Edge)
   - In Chrome, go to:  chrome://extensions   (Edge: edge://extensions)
   - Turn on "Developer mode".
   - Click "Load unpacked" and choose the folder
     C:\Users\<your name>\AnySave\extension
     (the installer opens it in Explorer for you).
   - Close Chrome completely and open it again.

4. Use it
   - Videos: open a video, click the AnySave icon (pin it from the
     puzzle-piece menu), pick a quality and click Download. Works on YouTube,
     Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and 1,000+ other sites.
   - Any link: right-click a link, video or image > "Download with AnySave".
   - Download manager: click the AnySave icon > "Download manager" to add any
     link (PDF, ZIP, MP3...), pause, resume and find your files.
   - Site download buttons: AnySave catches those downloads too and shows
     them in the manager (switch: "Catch downloads from websites").
   - Convert: click the AnySave icon > "Convert" to turn a video into MP3,
     make it smaller, or trim it.
   - Files are saved in  Downloads\AnySave, sorted into folders by type.
   - For Instagram, and Facebook posts that need a login, tick
     "Use my Chrome login".

   - Transcribe: in the download manager, the Transcribe button on a finished
     video turns its speech into text and subtitles (pick the language).

Why the warnings?  AnySave is free and not signed with a paid Microsoft
certificate, so Windows SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC" the
first time (also for AnySave-Setup.exe). "More info" > "Run anyway" is the
normal way to open such programs; nothing is wrong.

To remove AnySave:  double-click "Uninstall AnySave.bat" (More info > Run
anyway if asked). It asks first, and keeps your downloads.

Something stopped working?  Run "Install AnySave.bat" again. That gets the
newest version of the downloader, which fixes most problems.

Chrome may sometimes show "Disable developer mode extensions". Click the X
to keep AnySave working.

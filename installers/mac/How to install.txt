AnySave for Mac - download videos, files and convert media
==========================================================

1. Install the helper
   - Right-click "Install AnySave.command" and choose Open.
     (A normal double-click will say it's from an unidentified developer.
     If you only get a Done button: open System Settings > Privacy & Security,
     scroll down and click "Open Anyway".)
   - A Terminal window shows the progress. It downloads about 120 MB.
     Wait until it says "Done!".

2. Add the extension to Chrome
   - In Chrome, go to:  chrome://extensions
   - Turn on "Developer mode" (top right).
   - Click "Load unpacked" and choose the folder  AnySave > extension
     in your home folder (the installer opens it in Finder for you).
   - Quit Chrome completely (Cmd+Q) and open it again.

3. Use it
   - Videos: open a video, click the AnySave icon (pin it from the
     puzzle-piece menu), pick a quality and click Download. Works on YouTube,
     Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and 1,000+ other sites.
   - Any link: right-click a link, video or image > "Download with AnySave".
   - Download manager: click the AnySave icon > "Download manager" to add any
     link (PDF, ZIP, MP3...), pause, resume and find your files.
   - Site download buttons: AnySave catches those downloads too and shows
     them in the manager (switch: "Catch downloads from websites").
   - Convert: click the AnySave icon > "Convert" to turn a video into MP3,
     make it smaller, or trim it.
   - Files are saved in  Downloads > AnySave, sorted into folders by type.
   - For Instagram, and Facebook posts that need a login, tick
     "Use my Chrome login".

   - Transcribe: in the download manager, the Transcribe button on a finished
     video turns its speech into text and subtitles (pick the language).

Why the warnings?  AnySave is free and not signed with a paid Apple developer
certificate, so macOS warns the first time. Right-click > Open (or "Open Anyway"
in Privacy & Security) is the normal way to open such apps; nothing is wrong.

To remove AnySave:  right-click "Uninstall AnySave.command" and choose Open.
It asks first, and keeps your downloads.

Something stopped working?  Run "Install AnySave.command" again. That gets
the newest version of the downloader, which fixes most problems.

Chrome may sometimes show "Disable developer mode extensions". Click the X
to keep AnySave working.

## 1. What VidGrab is for

VidGrab is a personal tool for saving videos **you are allowed to save**, for example:

- videos you made or uploaded yourself
- videos whose owner has given you permission
- videos published under a licence that allows downloading (such as Creative Commons) or in the public domain
- uses your local law allows, such as personal backup or fair use / fair dealing

## 2. Copyright warning

Most videos on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok are protected by copyright and belong to the people who made them.
**Downloading, copying, re-uploading, sharing, selling or publicly showing someone else's video without permission can

of the site you downloaded from, which can get your account suspended.

Copyright laws differ from country to country. If you are not sure whether you may download a video, **don't**.
Ask the owner, or ask a lawyer.

## 6. Privacy

- VidGrab has **no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking**. The author's servers receive nothing from you.
- Videos are downloaded directly from the website to your device.
- If you log in to a site (Android) or tick "Use my Chrome login" (desktop), the login cookies stay **on your device**.
  They are sent only to that same website. On desktop they are written to a temporary file that is deleted as soon as
  the download finishes.
- VidGrab connects to GitHub to download and update yt-dlp (and, on desktop, ffmpeg).

## 7. Copyright complaints

videos, so infringing copies are on the user's own device and the original stays on the website it came from.
